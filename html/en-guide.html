Guide

  1. Open the app. You can create folders and documents without an account.
  2. Make a folder, then a document inside it. Folders can contain subfolders. Names cannot repeat at the same level.
  3. Write. Bold, size, color, quotes, lists, and indent are available. Undo works.
  4. Insert an image, or drop one into the editor. Double-click an image to zoom.
  5. For text you reuse, insert a copy block. It has a short title. One tap copies the body.
  6. Export checks folders or documents to an .nnb file. On import, choose the destination folder.
  7. Sign in only to upload or sync to the community. Registration uses email and a code. Notes written while signed out are not uploaded.

Language and appearance

The app switches between Simplified Chinese and English, and between light and dark.

Move and reorder

A document can move to another folder. Order in the library can be changed by dragging.
