Privacy

NoteYourNeed is a personal note-taking app. If you do not sign in to sync, it makes no network connections. Notes stay on the device.

We do not recommend storing extremely sensitive secrets here, such as seed phrases.

After you sign in, upload and community sync are manual. A local guest's notes cannot be uploaded. Registration uses email and a verification code.
